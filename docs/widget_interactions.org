* Purpose & scope

Every dialog, overlay, flyout, and every member of the absorbed
button/checkbox/chip/row/notice/strip/header families in the UI is an
instance of a base widget under =crates/taskboard-ui-slint/ui/widgets/=
(round-9 chunks 3, 12–17). Ad-hoc re-declaration of any grammar this
document covers is forbidden — a new mount that re-declares a
backdrop/panel pair, a button activation block, a caption+value row,
a tone-coloured notice, or a menu-row anatomy instead of mounting the
base widget is a review-blocker (lint-by-review until a lint exists).
This document is the taxonomy, the per-type contracts, and the sizing
defaults; the implementations live in the widget files cited per
section. The taxonomy descends from the inventory report
=.artifacts/reports/2026-11-18-round9-widget-inventory-centralization-report.md=
(§3 taxonomy, §4 sizing, §5 migration order).

Layering: =theme.slint= tokens → L1 primitives (=ui/widgets/=) →
L2 compositions (=ui/compositions/=) → L3 views. Every widget below is
presentation-only, callbacks-only intents, no business logic, and no
key handlers beyond the sanctioned leaf raw-key half (Return/Space on
activation widgets, the ladder keys in dropdown) per AGENTS §1/§4.

* Widget taxonomy

** Overlay hosts

- *Modal dialog* — =widgets/modal-dialog-host.slint::ModalDialogHost=.
  Scrim + dismissal + centered panel + the button model
  (=DialogButtonModel/=DialogButtonRole=, =DialogKind=) + walk arming.
  Customization points: =title=, =body-preferred-width= (defaults to
  =Theme.dialog-min-width=), =walk-stops=, =buttons=, =kind=
  (=edit= vs =confirm= — the R-4 outside-click resolution),
  =initial-focus-index=, =viewport-floor=, =open=; seams
  =button-invoked(int)=/=accepted()=/=dismissed()=. Mount protocol:
  =if (site-open-flag): ModalDialogHost { open: true; ... }= as a
  =key-root= sibling in the modal-class block, the site wiring the
  seams to its adapter; the site fires =closed= in its dismissal
  routing before clearing the flag. The button rows render
  =TextButton= (the J2 W-1 amend, landed with chunk 13).
- *Anchored overlay* — =widgets/anchored-overlay.slint= carries three
  symbols: =AnchoredOverlay= (the pure geometry helper: anchor facts
  in, placed rect out; =computed-x/y/flipped= observation seams;
  =OverlayPlacement=), =AnchoredOverlayHost= (the window-level host:
  underlay + panel + walk arming via =MaskWalkFacts=; customization
  points =anchor-x/y/width/height=, =viewport-width=,
  =viewport-floor=, =placement=, =panel-preferred-width= (defaults to
  =Theme.menu-min-width=), =walk-stops=, =open=; light-dismiss intent
  =dismissed()= — the unmount stays the site's flag and the adapter
  restores opener focus in its dismissal routing), and =AnchorMirror=
  (the zero-render producer-side mirror: bind =source-x/y/width/height=
  to the anchor's rects and the =published= callback; absorbs the
  #954 per-site =changed absolute-position= idiom — one copy instead
  of the former ~7). Anti-ad-hoc rule: re-declaring the
  backdrop/panel sibling pair outside the hosts is a review-blocker.
  Probe: =tests/widget_framework_probe.slint= + =_tests.rs=
  (G-1..G-13).

** Flyouts, dropdowns, menus

- *Dropdown* — =widgets/dropdown.slint::Dropdown= (the de-sited
  mount law, round-9 chunk 15): a closed field + chevron whose flyout
  renders window-level from the class facts global =DropdownFacts=.
  Identity is the typed =DropdownConsumer= enum pushed at the open
  edge — the spike gate (T-0) proved Slint 1.18.1 cannot self-register
  per-component handlers on a global callback (no parse rule for a
  qualified =Global.callback =>=; the two-way alias route refuses a
  handler), so ONE generic =AnchoredOverlayHost= mount in
  =app-window.slint= dispatches the flyout's row intents on the
  consumer kind (no per-site mount arms, no =site: int=; a new
  consumer adds one enum arm and zero app-window mount edits). The
  =dismissed()= contract (D-6): every widget-internal close (Escape,
  Tab, walk-away, the window-side fact close) routes the consumer's
  dismissal seam when the instance arms =has-dismiss-seam=, and stays
  the silent field-refocusing close otherwise; a flyout row-activated
  close selects first and never routes the dismissal
  (the =select-close= marker). Single-open class semantics: one
  =DropdownFacts.open= — two dropdowns cannot coexist statefully
  (research §4.3.2; homed as backlog). The selected flyout row renders
  the check glyph — a class-wide law (selected ⇒ emphasized text +
  check, =dropdown-flyout.slint::check-glyph=, D-5). The
  adapter-driven open rides =open-routes-external= + =open-requested()=
  (the #794 request-mirror idiom). The =DropdownTone= vocabulary
  STAYS a distinct enum (round-9 W6 D-7 ruling):
  =Semantic.tone-color='s =Tone= ladder has no =accent= arm
  (=.accent → Semantic.focus-ring=), and unification would grow the
  Tone vocabulary for exactly one consumer — the
  anti-speculative-generality law says stay. Probe:
  =tests/probes/dropdown_probe_tests.rs=.
- *Menu shell* — =widgets/menu-shell.slint::MenuShell= (chrome:
  raised surface, focus-ring border, drop shadow, clip, the row-count
  height cap behind =cap-rows=) + =widgets/menu-row.slint::MenuRow=
  (the one row anatomy: the =highlighted=-driven hover-union
  background — the consumer binds the union of its ladder/highlight
  match and the row's =hover-now=; busy-over-glyph leading slot with
  the collapse law; the check glyph only where =selected= is pushed —
  the dropdown class's law, never on menus; the KbdHint wrapper,
  the submenu chevron, the =text-role= typography, the right-button
  context seam and the tooltip-rect hover payload). Ladder ownership
  is per consumer contract and never merges into the shell: the
  dropdown's keys stay at the field's scope, the quick-list's at its
  panel FocusScope, and the action menu has none (the cursor is
  actions, D3). The a11y posture is the instantiation's (Slint 1.18
  keeps =accessible-role= constant): button + label + default action
  on menu rows, list-item on dropdown rows, none on quick-list rows.
  =dropdown-flyout.slint::DropdownFlyout= and
  =quick-list-flyout.slint::QuickListFlyout= inherit =MenuShell=;
  ActionMenu renders =MenuRow= internally (rows only) and keeps its
  composition machinery, placement and fill-to-floor height law at
  =compositions/action-menu.slint=. Anti-ad-hoc rule: a menu-row
  grammar outside =ui/widgets/menu-row.slint= is a review-blocker;
  the labels roster rows are the recorded form-row stay. Probe:
  =tests/probes/menu_shell_probe_tests.rs=.

  =compositions/recur-editor.slint= (the weekday toggles),

* The clip-focus law (#1419, operator ruling 2026-11-25)

Ruling: `https://github.com/D4id4los/taskboard/issues/1419#issuecomment-7172599685`
(option A of the plan at
=.artifacts/plans/2026-11-25-clip-sentinel-law-plan.md= §4). The law,
in force from 2026-11-25:

** Option A — clipped focus auto-scrolls the nearest scrollable ancestor

Focusing an element inside a clipped container is legal and the
container owes the user visibility: the nearest scrollable ancestor
seeks so the focused element enters the viewport (the A5a
=seek-page= hint-consumer precedent, commit 83e5f7af, generalized;
the board columns row's Flickable wrap + per-column seek consumption
is the application — fead35c2).

The refresh-synchronous focus drop inside a clipped subtree is a
*synthetic unmount echo, not a dismissal*: a model push pass can
unmount/remount the focused text field (a rebuild does what the bare
layout diffed away), and the teardown fires =text_flow_focus_lost=.
The absorb seam (=adapter.rs= =on_text_flow_focus_lost=, the
=refresh_push_in_progress= echo window armed at every push pass and
=apply_outcomes=) absorbs the echo — the FIRST focus-lost of a window
is that push's echo (a field that lost its mount cannot lose focus
twice), absorbed and disarmed in one step; every later focus-lost is
a genuine settle. The flag also disarms on any user-intent seam (the
seam macro arms) — a genuine interaction un-arms before its own
focus move can be mis-absorbed. The law also has a *remount half* —
a TextField left focusless by a push rebuild re-grabs (a
refocus-nonce-class mechanism) — which is PART OF THE LAW but NOT
YET LANDED: the first implementation (the =EditingFacts.refocus-nonce=
bump) was withdrawn in stage 2 (it re-grabbed a visible-but-unfocused
autofocus field mid-handover and raced the focus handover), and the
remaining requirement rides the wrap re-land (#1490, which also
carries the diagnosis of why the wrap's rebuild defeats every
restoration mechanism tried so far).

- *Enforcement seam*: two pins together — (1) the clip-focus
  regression (=view_component_tests::editing_dispatches_nothing_and_exits_via_escape=,
  the "mid-edit refresh keeps the flow open" case) must pass *with*
  the Flickable wrap present; (2) the seek probe
  (=columns_seek_probe_tests.rs= — a landing in a clipped column
  moves the columns element's window x negative, clamped to the
  content; the fitting regime stays at rest). Both wrap-dependent
  pins are WITHDRAWN with the wrap (see the status below); the pin
  set re-lands with it.
- *Walk-ladder interaction*: a focus walk that crosses into a clipped
  subtree continues through it — the seek is the walk's
  responsibility, so the ladder never silently strands focus in an
  unreachable region.
- *What the user sees*: the focused element is always visible;
  clipped columns are reachable by keyboard focus and pointer alike.

** Implementation status (2026-11-26, round 8 L1 stage 2/3)

The absorb seam (the echo window + the flow-aware
absorb-and-disarm) is LANDED. The refocus-nonce plumbing is NOT:
its bumps re-grabbed a visible-but-unfocused autofocus field
mid-handover on flowless pushes, so stage 2 dropped the mechanism
entirely (consumer, bumps and the =EditingFacts= mirror); the
remount half of the law stays open with the wrap re-land (#1490).
The Flickable wrap is WITHDRAWN a second time (fead35c2 → f0afe0e6,
then this chunk's re-land → withdrawal): the wrap's model-push
rebuild defeats the TextField init focus grab — the old instance's
deferred focus release lands after the new instance's init focus,
leaving the window with no focus item, and the rebuilt vi-insert
flow dead-ends the keyboard (Escape included). Every in-scope
restoration mechanism tried (EditingFacts-count-guarded refocus,
changed-width mount-settle re-grab, the refocus nonce with and
without the has-focus guard) leaves
=keymap_adapter_tests::insert_intent_opens_the_create_row_and_escape_returns=
red; per AGENTS §5 the wrap is withdrawn with the failing pin rather
than weakened — the pin itself is unweakened and passes with the
wrap absent. The full diagnosis and fix prompt ride #1490; the wrap
re-lands on top of the landed absorb seam once that refocus defect
is solved. Until then the board keeps decision 6's recorded clip
fallback, and the four B-board C1 signatures
(=board-0.5-600=, =board-3.0-600=) keep the rubric's fix-now grade —
the grade never silences the detector — recorded as accepted legacy

* Sizing defaults

All values are Theme token expressions (=Npx * base-scale=, the
=theme.slint= multiplier pushed from =[ui] scale=), never bare
pixels (the token lint). The width floor/cap pairs are
=theme.slint::dialog-min-width= / =dialog-max-width= and
=menu-min-width= / =menu-max-width=; the R-6 binding identity is
=min(viewport - 2·space-2, min(cap, max(preferred, floor)))=.

| Family | Default | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Click targets | 32 px (=space-6=) square pointer minimum — above WCAG 2.2 AA (24 px), below the touch-platform bars (44/48); the kiosk is a passive display (AGENTS §1) and base-scale raises everything on high-density screens; the 44/48 =touch-friendly= mode stays backlog until touch hardware is a target | =icon-button.slint::IconButton= |
| Text buttons | regular = space-6, compact = space-5, CTA = space-6 with min-width 4·space-6; height = =max(floor, content)= | =text-button.slint::TextButton= |
| Dialogs | min-width 280 px, max-width 560 px, preferred = content; height = content capped at =viewport-floor - 2·space-4=; never fixed caps, no vertical stretch | =theme.slint::dialog-min-width= |
| Menus / flyouts | min-width 200 px, max-width 360 px, row height = space-6, cap visible rows at 6 (=cap-rows=), panel max-height = available height | =theme.slint::menu-min-width= |
| Anchored overlays | flip + clamp into =[space-2, edge - space-2]=, never intersect the anchor rect, shrink to available space | =anchored-overlay.slint::AnchoredOverlay= |
| Text fields | single-line min-width = space-15 (96 px, the content-free floor rule, r2 D22) | =text-field.slint::TextField= |
| Chips | =Badge= space-1 padding/badge typography; =ActionChip= geometry via =chip-min-*= (default content-sized) | =action-chip.slint::ActionChip= |
| Ghost openers | row = space-6 + 2·space-1 band; column = space-6 + space-2 sliver, full height | =ghost-opener.slint::GhostOpener= |

Sourcing note: these encode the framework survey in inventory §4
(Material 3 / Apple HIG / WCAG 2.2 / Qt QStyle–QDialog–QComboBox /
MDN Popover, =<dialog=, anchor-positioning) — the inventory report
carries the links; this document carries only the landed values.

* Per-type interaction contract (the four laws)

1. *Positioning* — modals center (the capture expression, promoted);
   anchored overlays place below the anchor, flip above when the below
   rect would cross the viewport floor, clamp both axes into
   =[space-2, edge - space-2]=, and never intersect the anchor rect.
   The underlying layout never shifts (J2 R-7/R-8).
2. *Sizing* — caps are bounds, never targets: =min(viewport - 2·space-2,
   min(cap, max(preferred, floor)))=. Width floors/caps are Theme
   tokens (dialog 280/560, menus 200/360, ×base-scale); height is by
   content, capped at =floor - 2·space-4=; no vertical-stretch in any
   host.
3. *Dismissal* — inside clicks never close (the absorbing panel root);
   outside click resolves by kind (R-4): =edit=-kind dialogs treat
   outside click as accept/save, =confirm=-kind as cancel/dismiss.
   Destructive drafts discard only through the cancel seam. The
   right-button-down half rides the r4 D2 idiom. Anchored overlays are
   light-dismiss (no scrim); modals carry the scrim (R-3, =Theme.scrim=).
4. *Focus* — mounting through a host arms the walk mask
   (=MaskWalkFacts.extent = walk-stops=, cursor 0); the walk stays
   inside the dialog; on unmount the adapter restores focus to the
   opener through the existing FocusFacts seam (PR #872 machinery).
   The host has no key handlers — Escape resolves through the root
   capture + action layer.

The R-4 kind table: text/field editing dialogs (capture, recur) are
=edit=-kind candidates at migration; confirms (remove, discard) are
=confirm=-kind. Capture keeps =confirm= semantics until W2 decides
otherwise (behavior-preserving W1).

* Interactable-row cursor heuristic (J3 R-7)

Every widget with interactable rows carries a selected-row cursor.
The cursor is adapter-owned presentation state keyed by the row's
stable id (never an index), armed by the first vertical directional
stroke, clamped at refresh against the pass's id table, cleared on
view exit and every foreign settle; pointer activation moves it; row
verbs dispatch through the row's own seams, acting on the cursor'd
row. Adopted: the rule list (round 9 chunk 12, 2026-11-19 —

* Usage rules

- Dialog vs popover vs inline: a modal for focus-trapped decisions;
  an anchored overlay for light-dismiss pickers/menus; in-layout
  swaps only when no reflow (J2 R-2 — overlays never resize the view).
- Destructive actions never ride outside-click or autofocus; an
  irreversible action routes through =ConfirmStrip= or an =edit=-kind
  modal.
- Buttons: dismiss-role clusters left, accept/destructive right; the
  destructive role never takes autofocus. New clickable affordances
  are =TextButton= or =IconButton= variants — never a hand-rolled
  FocusScope+TouchArea block.
- *The knob-consumers rule*: every customization point on a base
  widget has ≥1 live consumer — no speculative generality. A knob
  whose last consumer disappears is deleted in the same change that
  removes the consumer (the stage-3 review gate enforces this; the
  =Anti-ad-hoc= siblings above are the same law on the mount side).
- One-open-overlay exclusivity: the newest host mounts above the
  older one (sibling order); the palette/menu grammar stays the
  one-modal-at-a-time rule.
- Tone→colour is =theme.slint::tone-color= / =tone-fill= — the
  canonical ladders (chunk 16; #952 closed). Widgets call the
  functions; no conditional Tone chains outside =theme.slint=
  (=DropdownTone.accent → Semantic.focus-ring= is the recorded
  exception).

* Base implementations & the mount checklist

The hosts are =widgets/modal-dialog-host.slint::ModalDialogHost= and
=widgets/anchored-overlay.slint::AnchoredOverlayHost= (plus the pure
=AnchoredOverlay= geometry helper and the =AnchorMirror= producer
mirror). =MaskWalkFacts= integration: the host owns extent/cursor;
the composition keeps only its own focus grab; =armed= stays
adapter-owned.

The six steps a new dialog site performs:

1. Declare the open flag in the model (the unmount condition).
2. Mount =if (flag): <Host> { open: true; ... }= at the correct
   sibling position (anchored block, then modal block — later siblings
   win the hit test).
3. Inject =viewport-floor= (=status.y=) and the anchor facts (via
   =AnchorMirror= for anchored sites).
4. Wire the intents (=accepted/dismissed/button-invoked/closed=) to
   the existing adapter seams; the site fires =closed= in its
   dismissal routing before clearing the flag.
5. Declare =walk-stops= and the =initial-focus= index; delete any
   per-widget mask ritual.
6. Declare the button model (empty for button-less dialogs).
