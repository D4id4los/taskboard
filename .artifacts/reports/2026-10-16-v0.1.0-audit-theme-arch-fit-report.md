## 11. Cross-walkthrough synthesis

Patterns that recur across ≥2 walkthroughs (observations per PD-8;
each stands on the finding rows above, no new claims):

1. **The lockstep ceremony is the workspace's per-feature tax**
   (W1, W3, W7): every domain-entity extension crosses ≥10 named
   sites in 5 crates (F16-01). The enums are compiler-protected; the
   three parallel structs (`TaskChanges`, `PendingTaskIntent`,
   `TaskClocks`) are not — a one-seam lockstep test is the cheapest
   prep any of the three waves could amortize.
2. **Identity is the recurring missing primitive** (W2, W3, W4, W8):
   batch identity (F16-12), undo-unit correlation (F16-13), board
   addressing (F16-05), occurrence identity (solved — F16-10's
   deliveries precedent), document-schema version (deck §7, consumed
   into F16-02). The data plane carries ids everywhere
   (`op_id`, `op_seq`, `(reminder, occurrence)`, uuid PKs); the
   control plane has almost none.
3. **Every seam the waves widen is a singleton, and each has exactly
   one widening point** (W2, W7, W8): SyncStatus (three layers,
   F16-22 — the only P1), "the bound board" (`live_board`, F16-06),
   the notification lane (F16-19), the single-target sync actor
   (F16-05). Meanwhile everything underneath (maps, per-board
   columns, per-board validator keys, N-rule reminders, the actor
   fan-in) is already plural — the friction concentrates in the
   narrow control/status vocabulary, not the data plane (F16-25,
   F16-17, F16-21, F16-03, F16-14 are all the same through-line:
   foundations laid, vocabulary narrow).
4. **Shared prep two waves could amortize**: the document machinery
   (W1 branch B) is exactly W5's sidecar branch (deck §4
   consequences) — one implementation, two decisions; the
   board-parameter shape (F16-23) is D2's and the multi-board wave's
   single decision; the addressing home (F16-05) is the registry
   step's and the substrate connector's. The vocabulary fork
   (synthesize-Deck-shapes vs widen-the-port, F16-08/F16-15) is one
   decision point serving D2 and D5 both.
5. **Churn-hotspot overlap**: five of nine walkthroughs land work in
   `commands/mod.rs` (36 touches, top of `§churn`) and three in
   `pipeline.rs` (14) — the two hottest files are exactly where
   multi-board (W8) and undo (W4) concentrate; remediation planning
   should expect those files to be contended across waves.
