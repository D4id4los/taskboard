## 3. The cross-milestone contract (the one shared seam)

The `nextcloud-files-sync` milestone's first consumer is this wave's
journal. The contract, binding on DR-7:

1. **Local-only home**: a domain entity + sqlite table + (per R-1) **no
   appdata sidecar section, no board attachment, no new sync lane**.
   Nothing journal-shaped enqueues `UpdateAppData`.
2. **Roaming-ready shape** (so the substrate's import is a pure fold,
   the #588 precedent's shape at entity scale): `BTreeMap` home keyed
   by a roaming-family UUID (v7); embedded per-entry mutation clock;
   deletes are tombstone-shaped rows (or tombstone-derivable), never
   silent absence; hand-written tolerant serde for any future wire row;
   A1 newtypes at the trust boundary; unknown-field tolerance in any
   serialized form.
3. **Vocabulary**: `journal` is **taken** — `taskboard-domain/src/journal.rs`
   is the undo command journal (`JournalEntry`, `JournalId`,
   `JournalState` are exported names). The round rules a
   non-colliding vocabulary (e.g. `reflection.rs` / `ReflectionEntry`)
   and records it; nothing may shadow or overload the undo names.
4. **The import-fold expectation** (options report §7): per-entry
   clock LWW union across devices' files; append-only entries union
   cleanly — R-5's editability ruling keeps edits/deletes possible, so
   tombstone discipline is not optional even device-local.

## 5. Feature directions (what each chunk's plan must settle)

### 5.1 DR-7 — journal & review design round (#621, #218 constraint)

**Mission**: the reflection anchor — the entry family (free text +
structured attributes: mood/energy ratings, curated health flags R-5),
prompt templates (curated set v1, P7), the boundary-generic review
walk (R-2: inbox triage → P9 neutral-copy reschedule → tomorrow's
boxes → prompted entry), gentle day-boundary surfacing (R-7), CLI legs
(`journal` verb family over the daemon) + export legs (VF-3).

**The round settles** (beyond §3's contract): vocabulary (VF-3
collision), entity shape + attribute representation (curated enums
with tolerant wire), template representation (data, not config;
roaming-ready too), the review surface shape (flow vs view; how
#622's planner is reached as the final leg), the gentle-surfacing
mechanism (banner/card riding VF-4's tick, P12 posture), editability +
undo arms (VF-7), cadence parameterisation mechanics (window
derivation per cadence), CLI verb set, kiosk posture (expectation:
passive at most), and the chunk mapping (the round opens its chunk
issues, DR-5 §7 precedent).

**Non-goals**: roaming (§3), statistics consumption (#623's slice),
weekly/monthly *interactive* sessions (#218 post-v0.2), health-flag
trend mining beyond what #623 later adds, LLM-assisted prompts.

### 5.2 Plan-next-day surface (#622)

Interactive placement (R-6) over the next day window: candidate pool
(scheduled/due tomorrow + picks from the live pool), write targets are
the landed `scheduled`/`effort` fields (VF-9), placement against the
day with boxes (P4) but **no arithmetic** (R-6). Standalone surface v1
(a view or flow + action; the round-agnostic shape is a stage-1
decision); the review walk integrates it later as its final leg (the
seam: reachable-by-command, no shared state beyond AppState).
Day-window aware (VF-4). Not an organiser view (W3) — no calendar, no
horizon beyond the next day. Kiosk: passive display only, if any.

### 5.3 Statistics & past-entries (#623, two slices)

**Slice A (dispatchable now)** — extends the Review view (VF-1) beyond
effort calibration: tracked-time aggregates over the 7/30-day windows
(Work vs Respite split per the landed `TimeEntryKind`; per-day totals
via `tracked_in_window`), focus-session readings as **Work-block
counts/durations** (the round-free honest semantics — entries carry no
focus-mode marker; the projection names Work blocks, and the
limitation is recorded, not papered over), hand-rolled bars/sparklines
(R-3) fed by pure `presentation` values. Skeleton discipline: the
D-8/D-9 tiers keep holding as sections grow.
**Slice B (gated on #621)** — reflection-entry trends (mood/energy/
health flags over windows), past-entries browsing, and the journal
export leg (VF-3). The Review home stays one surface.

### 5.4 PDF export & print (#624, two stages)

**Stage A — crate analysis (research chunk)**: candidates evaluated
for the ruled layout set (single-sided A4/A5/A6 + fold imposition with
mirrored panel order; R-4), maintenance/support posture, update
cadence, MSRV/dependency weight, licensing vs the dual-tier model,
memory footprint (kiosk-class targets), text-shaping/i18n needs;
exactly one recommendation; evidence & artefact mandate.
**Stage B — implementation (gated on stack approval)**: fixed curated
layouts (R-4) rendering from the today projection (VF-9);
PDF-to-viewer delivery (open with the system handler); destination =
explicit path (config default); a file-writing `export`-family leg
(VF-3's explicit extension); kiosk builds exclude the feature (and
ideally the dependency) — feature-gate ruled at design time; print
beyond viewer hand-off stays fenced. The layout mini-design (section
order, phrase vocabulary) rides the today projection's phrases.

### 5.5 Focus follow-ups (#600)

The two fenced v1 gaps as one coherent design: **auto-chain + every-N
long-break cadence** (needs the focus runtime's first owned memory — a
`LadderMemory`-shaped per-session counter; D-F1's statelessness is
superseded *for this knob only*, recorded) and **phase-boundary
notifications** (toast/chime at focus→break→idle transitions riding
the escalation driver's `ChimeSink`/notification templates, VF-6;
P12 gentle defaults, insistence by consent). New `[focus]` knobs follow
the plain-minutes config pattern; #625's editor grows rows for them.
Kiosk exclusion stands.

### 5.6 Focus timings settings editor (#625)

Settings-view fields for `[focus]` timings (and #600's knobs when they
land) over the landed config-persist path; typed
`ConfigError::InvalidFocusTiming` surfacing as field validity (the
settings-form pattern); commit-on-release for numeric steppers (the
scale precedent); no new config semantics. Small, sequenced after #600
(territory).

### 5.7 Tracker reminders (#504)

`ReminderRef::Tracker(HabitId)` + occurrence semantics (the round-free
leaning: `anchor_time` as the daily-nudge anchor, stable-instant
occurrence keys per VF-5), the `due_firings` arm, re-arm on period
rollover by construction, DR-3 insistence interplay. **Roam ruling
leaning** (record for the stage-1 plan): habits and their facts are
board-roaming (DR-6); tracker reminder *rules* ride the same sidecar
family, dismissals/proofs stay per-device — consistent with the
landed rules/dismissals split. Kiosk: firing surfaces render; editing
desktop-side.

### 5.8 Scroll anchoring (#592, optional)

Measure-first (the chunk may conclude "decline"): optional row-anchored
compensation on above-viewport changes in the in-house ListView; cost
= extra extent measurement + possible fight with the repeater's own
re-anchoring. Last in the wave; drop without ceremony if the
measurement says no.

## 8. Risks

- **DR-7 slips** → chunks 9/11 slip with it; the wave's other seven
  chunks are unaffected (deliberate). Mitigation: dispatch chunk 1
  first.
- **Vocabulary collision** (VF-3) escaping the round → compile-level
  confusion between undo-journal and reflection-journal. Mitigation:
  the contract names it; the round's first decision is the naming
  ruling.
- **PDF crate ruling stalls** (no candidate fits fold imposition
  within licensing/footprint) → stage B waits; the analysis chunk must
  state the fallback (e.g. imposition by layout parameterisation over
  a simpler canvas API) rather than an empty recommendation.
- **Review-view growth** past its skeleton discipline as slices stack
  → visual/layout drift. Mitigation: slice A pins the D-8/D-9 tiers
  as its structural tests before adding sections.
- **Two milestones, one implementer** (the options report's devil):
  attention split. Mitigation: the seams are contractual (§3), the
  files-sync round owns its own artefacts; no chunk here reads
  files-sync state.
- **#592 measurement says no** → drop quietly; the issue records the
  measurement and stays honest.
