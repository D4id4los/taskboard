## 7. Risks

- **ADR/migration drift** (0020 already collided once): every chunk
  re-verifies counters at its own base; single-flight per batch.
- **Three parallel streams, one implementer** (this round, files-sync
  #631+, GUI review round 5): the §3 territory rules + rebase-on-start
  discipline; the GUI stream owns ui-slint chrome, this round's 3–4
  own the review/walk surfaces.
- **krilla 0.x cadence** (bus factor ≈ 1): the feature gate contains
  the blast radius; the pdf-writer fallback is a one-level port.
- **Slice-B scope creep** (trend mining is explicitly fenced): the
  chunk's acceptance is browsing + trend phrases over the curated
  attributes, nothing adaptive.
- **#638's M/L size**: the walk is the wave's largest single surface;
  if it slips, the milestone still completes functionally via 1–2 +
  4–5 (the walk is the crown, not the foundation).
